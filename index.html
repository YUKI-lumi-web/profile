02 / ABOUT

而是细腻的洞察力。

If the heart is a can.An organ taken from a human body,I want to put my hand into my chest.Take it out.I am going to wash it with warm water and dry it with a towel.Air it in a sunny and well-ventilated place.

我的工作方式很直接：找到真正值得表达的核心， 再用尽量少的元素把它成立。比起堆叠效果， 我更在意节奏、留白和观看时的呼吸感。
